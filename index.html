Arrows/WASD/QEZC move · Space wait · R rest · X scent · click or tap the map to travel · bump to attack · right-click or long-press a monster to inspect it.

Drag the map (mouse or finger) or Shift+Arrows to look around. Pinch or scroll to zoom. The view stays where you put it until you press Center view.

Red warning tiles show an attack that resolves on your next action. Step aside or stun its source.

Descend 20 floors in four stages of five. Each stage starts in a waste room stocked with a few free items. The last stage is human.

Your starting limbs are weak and rot very fast. Stronger grafts decay faster. Stored grafts decay at 25% speed. Legs set your speed: rotten legs or none at all slow you.
