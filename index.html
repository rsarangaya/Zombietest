Tap the map to travel, bump to attack, right-click or long-press a monster to inspect it. Drag to look around, pinch or scroll to zoom.

Tap an attached graft in your pack to Detach, Devour, or use its active ability (also shown on the action bar when ready).

Red warning tiles show an attack that resolves on your next action. Step aside or stun its source.

Descend 20 floors in four stages of five. Each stage starts in a waste room stocked with a few free items. The last stage is human.

Your starting limbs are weak and rot very fast. Stronger grafts decay faster. Legs set your speed.
